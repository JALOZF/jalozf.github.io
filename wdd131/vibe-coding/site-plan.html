Overview

Purpose

This site is intended to be a public portfolio, detailing the development, challenges, and successes of my coding projects. I utilize this platform to provide clear evidence of my technical proficiencies and my commitment to continuous professional learning.

Audience

My primary audience is potential employers and hiring managers who evaluate my practical skills, problem-solving methods, and technical communication abilities. My secondary audience includes professional peers and industry contacts seeking to track my work, offer constructive feedback, and engage in technical discourse.

Dynamic elements

Change site theme to accomidate light and dark mode device preferences, generate a list of pages with title and short description, and navigate between blog pages. Sort date and filter languages. (Python, HTML, CSS, JS, etc.)

Wireframes

Home page, an about page, and an example post.

Home

In the upper left corner will be the logo. The logo will change colors with the theme via dynamic CSS targeting an inline SVG. The filter and sort dropdowns at the top of the page will filter by language tags (HTML, JS, Python, etc.) and sort by date or alphabetical order respectively. The link 'Newest', will link to the newest page. Every card for a post will include an image, the title of the page, a short summary, and tags describing what languages were used. At the bottom, from right to left will be icons for contact, GitHub, and LinkedIn respectively. Like the nav, there will also be an image of the logo beside the test "Vibe Coding: Development Log".

About

The image beside the section titled 'who am I' will be a photo of myself. There may be additional sections below 'Why did I make this site' styles similar to that section.

Post

Each post page will be styled similar to conventional blog post, including sections displaying code, styled similar to the language it is written in.
